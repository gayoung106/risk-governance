# PART I.

## Submission Readiness Audit

Before opening the submission system, verify the following.

---

### Scientific Readiness

□ Research question is clearly stated.

□ Scientific gap is explicit.

□ Mechanism is theoretically justified.

□ Contribution is explicit.

□ Boundary condition is identified.

□ Practical implication follows directly from findings.

---

### Methodological Readiness

□ Variables correctly operationalized.

□ Questionnaire descriptions match actual survey items.

□ Reliability reported.

□ Validity discussed.

□ Robustness checks completed.

□ Missing data addressed.

□ Software reported.

□ Model assumptions checked.

---

### Writing Readiness

□ Title concise.

□ Abstract within journal limit.

□ Keywords appropriate.

□ Introduction problem-driven.

□ Discussion not repetitive.

□ References complete.

□ Tables correctly numbered.

□ Figures publication quality.

# PART XXVII.

## JRR Submission Principles

The reviewed corpus suggests the following principles:

Editors value scientific contribution over statistical novelty.

Mechanism is more important than variable count.

Transparency outweighs methodological complexity.

Construct validity is non-negotiable.

Policy implications must remain evidence-based.

Discussion should advance theory rather than repeat results.

---

# PART XXVIII.

## Final Author Checklist

Before submission ask

Can I explain

in one sentence

What is the problem?

What is the gap?

What is the mechanism?

What is the contribution?

Why JRR?

If any answer is unclear,

the manuscript is not yet ready.

---

# Final Principle

A JRR manuscript is ready for submission only when it satisfies three simultaneous conditions:

Scientific clarity.

Theoretical credibility.

Methodological transparency.

Meeting these three standards consistently characterised the uploaded JRR articles and should therefore guide all future submissions.
