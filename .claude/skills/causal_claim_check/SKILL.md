# Causal Claim Check

Purpose:
Prevent inappropriate causal claims in cross-sectional survey research.

Rules:

1. Avoid definitive causal wording.
2. Prefer:
   - "associated with"
   - "related to"
   - "linked to"
   - "suggests"

3. Be cautious when interpreting mediation.
4. Explicitly acknowledge:
   - cross-sectional limitations
   - self-report limitations
   - endogeneity possibility

5. Maintain balance:
   - avoid overclaiming
   - avoid contribution collapse

Goal:
Preserve credibility without weakening theoretical significance.
