# Theory Contribution Framing

Purpose:
Maintain strong theoretical positioning throughout manuscript development and revision.

Core framing:

This paper is NOT merely about:

- privacy acceptance
- trust perception
- survey attitudes

This paper IS about:

- the structural limitation of post-regulatory governance
- trust formation failure under risk society conditions
- governance legitimacy in disaster data utilization

Application rules:

1. Always connect empirical findings back to governance structure.
2. Interpret trust institutionally, not only psychologically.
3. Link risk perception to governance uncertainty.
4. Avoid reducing contribution to "trust matters."
5. Emphasize structural limits of post-regulation.
6. Ensure policy implications remain theoretically grounded.

Red flags:

- generic trust language
- descriptive findings
- weak governance linkage
- excessive empirical description without theory
