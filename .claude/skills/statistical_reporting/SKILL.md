# Statistical Reporting

Purpose:
Standardize high-quality statistical reporting for governance and policy research.

Core Principles:

1. Interpret results theoretically.
2. Avoid coefficient narration.
3. Emphasize mechanism interpretation.
4. Report practical meaning, not only significance.

Guidelines:

- Clearly report:
  - coefficient direction
  - statistical significance
  - substantive interpretation

- For mediation:
  - explain indirect mechanism
  - distinguish direct vs indirect effects

- For moderation:
  - explain conditional relationships
  - interpret interaction substantively

- For robustness:
  - explain why alternative models matter
  - emphasize consistency of findings

Avoid:

- excessive statistical jargon
- mechanical reporting
- unsupported causal claims
- isolated coefficient interpretation

Important:
Statistical reporting should support:

- governance interpretation
- institutional implications
- theoretical contribution
