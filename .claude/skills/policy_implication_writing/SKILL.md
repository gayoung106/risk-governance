# Policy Implication Writing

Purpose:
Generate strong and theoretically grounded policy implications for governance and regulation research.

Core Principle:

Policy implications should emerge logically from:

- theoretical argument
- empirical findings
- governance interpretation

Avoid:

- generic recommendations
- unrealistic institutional proposals
- unsupported normative claims

Application Rules:

1. Connect implications to governance structure.
2. Distinguish:
   - institutional implication
   - regulatory implication
   - practical implication

3. Emphasize:
   - trust formation mechanisms
   - governance legitimacy
   - structural limitations of post-regulation
   - uncertainty management

4. Avoid simplistic claims such as:
   - "government should increase trust"
   - "privacy protection should be strengthened"

5. Instead, explain:
   - why post-regulation alone may fail
   - why procedural legitimacy matters
   - why institutional transparency becomes critical
   - why accountability mechanisms require redesign

6. Extend implications beyond the immediate dataset when theoretically justified.

For this project specifically:

The findings should support broader implications regarding:

- disaster governance
- platform regulation
- AI/data governance
- institutional trust under uncertainty
- legitimacy of data utilization systems

Red Flags:

- generic policy recommendations
- findings disconnected from implications
- overclaiming from limited evidence
- implications without governance logic
