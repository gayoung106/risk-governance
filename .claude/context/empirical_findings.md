# Empirical Findings

## Main Findings

1. manage_trust positively predicts consent.
2. institutional trust strongly predicts consent.
3. perceived risk negatively predicts consent.
4. safety perception alone has limited explanatory power.
5. trust mediates the relationship between management trust and consent.
6. interaction effects suggest trust becomes more important under high-risk conditions.

---

## Governance Interpretation

The findings suggest that:
formal post-regulatory safety management alone may not sufficiently generate policy acceptance.

Instead, institutional trust plays a central role in governance legitimacy under uncertainty.

---

## Important Interpretation Rule

Do NOT interpret findings merely psychologically.

Interpret findings institutionally:

- governance legitimacy
- institutional trust
- structural limitation of post-regulation
- uncertainty management
