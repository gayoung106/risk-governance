# Methodological Strengths

## Strengths

1. Multiple robustness checks conducted.
2. Alternative specifications tested.
3. Mediation and moderation both examined.
4. Interaction effects analyzed.
5. Subgroup analyses performed.
6. Effect sizes reported.
7. Diagnostic tests included.

---

## Important Limitation Framing

Acknowledge:

- cross-sectional limitation
- self-report limitation

However:
avoid collapsing contribution through excessive limitation statements.

---

## Reviewer Positioning

The study prioritizes:

- governance interpretation
- institutional explanation
- theoretical consistency

rather than purely predictive modeling.
