# Reviewer Risks

This document summarizes likely reviewer criticisms and strategic response considerations.

---

# Major Reviewer Risks

## 1. "This is just another privacy acceptance study."

### Risk

Reviewers may interpret the manuscript as:

- a generic trust survey
- a privacy calculus study
- a descriptive acceptance model

### Strategic Response

Continuously emphasize:

- governance legitimacy
- structural limitation of post-regulation
- risk society framing
- institutional trust

Avoid overly psychological interpretation.

---

## 2. Weak theoretical novelty

### Risk

Reviewers may argue:

- findings are predictable
- trust effects are obvious
- contribution is incremental

### Strategic Response

Emphasize:

- institutional interpretation of trust
- governance failure mechanisms
- post-regulatory limitation
- uncertainty governance

---

## 3. Risk society theory appears superficial

### Risk

Reviewers may criticize:

- symbolic usage of Ulrich Beck
- weak conceptual integration
- insufficient mechanism explanation

### Strategic Response

Explicitly connect:

- uncertainty
- technological risk
- institutional distrust
- governance legitimacy problems

---

## 4. Cross-sectional survey limitation

### Risk

Reviewers may criticize:

- lack of causal identification
- self-report bias
- common method bias

### Strategic Response

- avoid strong causal language
- use cautious interpretation
- emphasize theoretical consistency
- acknowledge methodological limitations without collapsing contribution

---

## 5. Single-item trust variable

### Risk

Reviewers may question:

- construct validity
- measurement reliability

### Strategic Response

- acknowledge limitation transparently
- justify practical survey constraints
- emphasize theoretical consistency

---

## 6. Policy implications are too broad

### Risk

Reviewers may criticize:

- overgeneralization
- unsupported governance claims

### Strategic Response

Ensure implications:

- emerge logically from findings
- remain institutionally grounded
- avoid exaggerated normative recommendations

---

# Critical Strategic Principle

The manuscript must consistently maintain:

This is NOT merely a study about privacy attitudes.

This is a study about:
the structural limitation of post-regulatory governance in generating institutional trust under risk society conditions.
