# Theoretical Positioning

## Core Theoretical Position

The manuscript positions disaster-related personal data governance as a governance legitimacy problem under risk society conditions.

The project argues that:
post-regulatory governance systems face structural limitations in generating institutional trust under uncertainty.

---

## Theoretical Foundations

### 1. Risk Society Theory

Based on Ulrich Beck:

- modern risks are uncertain
- technological systems generate new governance challenges
- institutional trust becomes unstable
- traditional governance mechanisms become insufficient

---

### 2. Privacy Governance

The manuscript interprets privacy governance not merely as:

- technical safety management
- legal compliance

but as:

- institutional legitimacy formation
- governance trust construction

---

### 3. Policy Acceptance

Policy acceptance is interpreted as:
a governance legitimacy outcome,
not merely an individual attitude.

---

## Core Mechanism

The manuscript assumes:

formal safety management alone may not sufficiently generate trust under uncertainty conditions.

As a result:
institutional trust becomes more important than perceived safety management itself.

---

## Important Positioning Rule

Do NOT interpret:
trust merely as an individual psychological perception.

Interpret trust as:
an institutional legitimacy mechanism within governance systems.
