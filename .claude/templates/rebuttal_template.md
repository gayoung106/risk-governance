# Rebuttal Template

## Reviewer Comment

Summarize reviewer concern clearly and respectfully.

---

## Response Strategy

Classify:

- accepted
- partially accepted
- strategically defended

---

## Revision Description

Clearly explain:

- what was revised
- where revisions were made
- why revisions improve the manuscript

---

## Contribution Protection

Ensure:

- revisions do not weaken contribution
- theoretical framing remains intact
- governance interpretation is preserved

---

## Tone Guidelines

Maintain:

- professional tone
- respectful tone
- confident tone

Avoid:

- defensive language
- excessive apology
- unnecessary concession
