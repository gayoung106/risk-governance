## 3. APA 7th formatting

**MINOR ISSUE**

Under APA 7th edition, in-text citations for works with **three or more authors** should be shortened to the first author's surname plus "et al." starting from the *first* citation (this differs from APA 6th, which permitted full listing on first mention). The following in-text citations currently spell out all authors and should be shortened:

- "Mayer, Davis, & Schoorman (1995)" / "(Mayer, Davis, & Schoorman, 1995)" → should read "Mayer et al. (1995)" / "(Mayer et al., 1995)" — appears in Introduction §1 and Theory §2.2 (twice).
- "(Xu, Dinev, Smith, & Hart, 2011)" → should read "(Xu et al., 2011)" — Theory §2.2.
- "(Mutimukwe, Kolkowska, & Grönlund, 2020)" → should read "(Mutimukwe et al., 2020)" — Theory §2.2.

The **reference-list entries themselves are correctly unabridged** (APA 7 requires full author lists up to 20 authors in the reference list), so this issue affects only in-text citation form, not the bibliography.

## 9. Page numbers

**MINOR ISSUE**
